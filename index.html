Why Synthesia?

AI is changing how companies create, personalise and scale content, and Synthesia is one of the companies defining that shift rather than reacting to it.

What makes the role especially interesting to me is the Product Marketing challenge: a fast-evolving product, multiple audiences and a category that is still being shaped in real time.

I’ve also heard consistently great things about the company and the people from friends who work there.

Why Product Marketing?

I’ve spent 7 years working at the point where product strategy meets customer behaviour.

I now want to move further upstream: not only translate positioning into high-performing customer experiences, but help shape the positioning and go-to-market strategy itself.

What I bring into that move is a strong feedback loop between messaging, customer intent, conversion and commercial impact.

You haven’t held a Product Marketing title. Why should we take the bet?

Because the core work isn’t new to me.

I already work across product understanding, customer research, competitive intelligence, positioning, launches, Sales collaboration and measurement.

What changes is the level of ownership: moving from owning the customer-facing and growth side of the equation to owning more of the upstream Product Marketing strategy as well.

What would you need to ramp up on?

Pricing and packaging strategy.

I’ve worked on the website implementation and competitive side of pricing, but I haven’t formally owned pricing and packaging decisions end-to-end.
